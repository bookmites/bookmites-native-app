# Bookmites Native App

The Bookmites native app is our contribution to the free open source software world. It seeks to be a private, free, phone-based epub reader that offers a premium user experience. 

## **Our Design Principles:**
--------------------------------------------------
*These are WIP*

### **Avoid the Panagoricon.**
In the web2 world, you are never not in a market. You cannot purchase a book and then retreat to read it. Rather, you’re always reading with a marketer over your shoulder watching you so they can make the next suggestion. It isn’t just a panopticon—where you’re always being watched. It is the infinite agora—you’re always being sold to. The app experience is not about deepening your reading experience—it is about making the next sale. 

In our app, you can retreat. Our library is accessible, but the purpose of our app is not to entice you to start the next book. 


### **FOSS doesn’t have to be janky**
We’d rather ship a high quality product with less features than a janky product with many features. 

Our goal is to enable people to read dense texts for long periods at a time in a completely focused manner. That isn’t pleasurable with a janky interface. 

### **Transparency**
We believe tools should be convivial: that requires them to be transparent. 

We don’t aspire to an app that feels “techie”, we use progressive disclosure to tuck away the technical workings of the app. However, we do design thoughtfully to find ways to gradually introduce the app’s functionality for users. 

All of our code is open-source and can be expected by more technical users. 

### **Data Sovereignty**
We believe users should own their data. That means two things:
We don’t have access to it
Users can access their data easily

We don’t have access to it
We design with a local-first mentality. Data is stored on a user’s device and backed up in their infrastructure. Data does not trickle back to us. We love it when users share their data with us to improve the product, but that only happens when users proactively decide to do so. When they do, we use best practices to anonymize and securely store that data.

Users can access their data.
We prioritize human-readable data. We want users to discover it, inspect it, and feel comfortable editing it. 


### **AI Transparency & Ownership**
We do not treat AI as a black magic box. We want users to understand what the AI is doing and how it works. 

Users can (must) bring their own AI
Our goal is not to be an intermediary between users and model providers. We simply seek to create a tool that is seamless for users to plug in their own models. 

Legible, Controllable Harness
Good quality AI depends on the system prompts that shape it. For a consumer for-profit app, this can be their “secret sauce”. For our app, we seek to make that visible, understandable, and controllable by the user.
