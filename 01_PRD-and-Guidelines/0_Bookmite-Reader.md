# **Overview**
The Bookmite Reader, referred to from here on as "The Reader," is a Free and Open Source (FOSS) epub reader. It is a product made by the Bookmites team (bookmites.org).

The goal of the reader is to create a high-quality ebook reading experience for mobile that:
- **Is accessible:** anyone can use it for free.
- **Is high quality:** is a better user experience than other readers
- **Is private:** enables users to read without corporations or governments (or anyone) tracking their reading. 
- **Is AI-empowered:** users can tackle harder books or reflect on their reading with an AI chatbot. 


**Support: Books**
The ereader supports Epub2 and Epub3 file types. It primarily supports text reading (multimedia outside images is not a priority).

**Support: AI**
The reader supports a "BYOK" approach to AI. It might also (later) offer a service to users where the Bookmites organization faclitates users with accounts (though that is much later). 

**Private, local-first**
The Bookmites organization does not proactively collect data on its users or their reading through the app. We support a "local first" approach to the ereader: reading progress, highlights, AI chats, and other user data are stored first on the phone locally. Users have the option to back up the app to the cloud.
