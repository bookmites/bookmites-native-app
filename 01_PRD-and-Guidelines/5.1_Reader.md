# **Overview: The Reader**
`Reader` is the epub reader component in our system: the part of the app where the user actually views an epub and can navigate through it. 


## **Reader View States**
The reader has two view states:
 **`read` State:** The state for actual, focused reading. The reading surface (the epub page) takes over the full screen. The user can highlight in this state, and later on will be able to take other actions (such as enter an AI chat).
- **`nav` State:** Navigation and settings. The "Reader Chrome" is visible, allowing the user to navigate out of the reader, and use other navigation interactions, such as table of contents, adjust reader settings, etc...



Specifically, what that means is:
 **`read`:** Focused reading. **`ReaderSurface`** fills the screen; **reader chrome** is hidden.
- **`nav`:** Navigation and settings. Reader chrome visible; **`ReaderSurface`** is centered, visually scaled to **80%**, with a gray border (transform shrink, no epub reflow).


## How it works

- **Toggle:** A **quick tap** on the visible book content (the **`ReaderSurface`** hit target—not a drag, not a long-press) switches modes.
  - `read` → quick tap → `nav`
  - `nav` → quick tap on the still-visible page → `read`
- **Open:** Always start in **`read`** when entering The Reader (no remembered mode).
- **Highlight edit:** Only in **`read`**. While editing a quote, quick-tap toggle is **disabled**. Quote edit cannot start in **`nav`**. Tapping an existing highlight from **`read`** does **not** enter **`nav`** (future highlight actions are separate).
- **Settings:** **`ReaderSettingsModal`** opens only from **`nav`** (settings control in reader toolbar). Dismiss (tap outside or close control) → modal closes, chrome stays in **`nav`**.
