**Overview: This is a Work in Progress**
The Bookmites app is a living application, we are continuously ading to it. 

*We are currently in: Phase 0.2*

------------------------------------------------------------

**Phase 0:**
Launch a React Native app on Android and iPhone that enables users to read a book.

**0.2: Read a book.**
Users can upload a book to a native app and read it in the reader. It tracks their location/progress (so they can open it again and keep reading). We support dyslexic font.

As a user, I can:
- *Upload a book from my files.* Add a book to the app from a local file folder. (Does this copy, or just remember the path?). Remove the book from Reader.
- *Read a book in the epub reader.* Users can open a book in the reader and read it.Turn pages. Retains their location from session to session. Navigate table of contents.
- *Change the reading font.* Toggle between multiple fonts, including dyslexic-friendly font. 
- *Highlight.* Highlight a passage in the book.
- *Remove a book from the app* Remove a book from being visible in the app.
- *Remove a book from being stored locally* IF the user has decided to not keep the book locally, but they still want to retain their progress, highlights, etc... this enables them to remove it from local storage without harming their metadata.
