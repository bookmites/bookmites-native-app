## **Philosophy of the app**
It is about sovreignty, ownership, and transparency. 
Everyone agrees that we want to build something that empowers people. It is about transparency for users, and giving them control and (in a progressive way) knowledge and understanding about how things work.

## **The plan**
I (nefko) have pushed us to think about the "first 6 months" as a frame. 
As a volunteer team, I know that we need to maintain momentum by shipping things that we're proud of, and we need to get people (even a small number) using and loving the MVP. Quality (at this stage) is more important than quantity. 

First six months gives us a time-bounded threshold where we can pull features into the "now" or push them into the "future", which helps us control scope. 

Personas are a language that I (nefko) speak best, but everyone understands them as an organizing principle or work. I feel confident with this tool because of my background. These personas are sketches, they're not based in research. However, they are "educated guesses" in the sense that I've done tengential resarch to this project in the past. 

The other thing is that the initial personas hone in on more tech-savvy users. The reason is this: there is a behavioral quality that we're looking for (a certain type of reader), and if we can lock that in with the more tech-savvy ones early on, we can get a little traction. We know how to lower barriers, but that is eng effort, and I want to achieve a little momenum and product market fit (PMF) first. Once we've hit that, we can focus on lowering tech barriers. 

I also just want to get some reps in for the team.
