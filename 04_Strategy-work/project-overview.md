**Bookmites is an open-source project that Nefko contributes to.**
It is a formation of a handful of people that he used to work with at an AI-reader company who was his client. Recall that Nefko is a professional US designer/researcher/strategist who works as a freelancer. Many of the people in Bookmites worked on the client-side as W2 employees at one of his clients. 

**Bookmites is an early-stage (ill-defined) project devoted to accessibility in reading.** 
The project overall is focused on accessibility in a broad sense:
- Dyslexic fonts and visual accessibility. Building a good e-reader for people with visual impairments. 
- Book ownership - actually being able to own ebooks (rather than promoting a rentier economy like Kindle).
- Accessibility of "grey-market" texts - trying t get lesser-known books to be accessible to a wider audience. 
- Better tools - better UX than things like Moonreader, Calibre, etc... Promoting Standard Ebooks by creating a high-quality reading experience. 

As of fall 2026, this is still pretty loose. 

**It has access to the Standard Ebooks catalogue**
Note that we have some data on the cataloue of standard ebooks. While the app will promote bring-your-own-book (BYOB), we'll also streamline accessibility of the 1,500 or so Standard Ebooks.
